Explained in 60 Seconds

Why Did The CI Build Fail?

A story about a guard robot, a VIP club, and a misread name tag.

1

The Rule: No Cooking From Raw Ingredients!

Cooking meals from scratch takes 4 hours. Buying pre-packaged meals takes 5 seconds.

2

The VIP List: Home-Made Snacks

Our little tools in pkgs/ are home-made. The guard robot has a scanner to read their VIP tags.

✅ Most Apps (Normal Tag)

They use the tag pname:

pname = "bun-canary";

Scanner read: "VIP Approved!"

❌ Calldiff (Different Tag)

It used the tag name:

name = "calldiff";

Scanner saw: NOTHING!

3

The False Alarm 🚨

Because the guard's scanner only looked for pname, it completely missed Calldiff's tag!

4

The Simple Fix: Teach The Scanner

We told the scanner robot to check for BOTH kinds of name tags!

# Before: Only looked for pname
- grep 'pname = "..."'

# After: Looks for pname OR name
+ grep '(pname|name) = "..."'
🎉 Scanner saw "name = calldiff" ➔ VIP Allowed ➔ CI Turned Green!
